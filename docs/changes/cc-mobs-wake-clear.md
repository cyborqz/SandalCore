# Changes of cc-mobs-wake-clear (a line that starts with # is a comment, and so are blank lines)

@known-issues set Combat / Mob debuffs
| **Combat / Mob debuffs** | A mob takes the debuffs of a hit, the Burning debuff of a hit that sets it on fire (the Mage's Fireball with the Fire Empowerment, the wand's Burning finisher): a fire number over it every second for 8 seconds (up to 24 with more burning hits), with the fire and the Burning icon on it. The Burning debuff goes on through a reset, ends with the mob's death, and ends when the player who lit it leaves or dies. While a mob walks home after a reset its damage-over-time debuffs still hurt it but start no fight; once it is home every damage-over-time debuff that is still on it starts the fight again, so an attacker it cannot see makes it leash and heal again at each tick until the debuff ends. The bleeds and poisons of a hit take Health from a mob at their ticks: a Bleeding debuff every second for 6 seconds, a Hemorrhage every half second for 5 seconds, a Poison every 2 seconds for 20 seconds with its stacks (up to ten) making each tick bigger (modelled, not confirmed: each tick counts every stack); a mob has one Bleeding debuff for each attacker, and a Hemorrhage only lands on a bleeding mob. The debuffs that change the mob's own numbers work: Shaken makes it take more physical damage and Volatile more magical damage, Demoralized makes it hit softer, Blinded makes it miss more, and a new hit adds time to them up to 15 or 20 seconds. Weakened and Dazed lower the mob's physical and magical speed numbers, but a mob does not attack slower for them yet. Slows and snares (Chilled, Snared, the Tank's Grapple, mud and webs) work: a mob walks at its speed times one minus the strongest snare on it, at once, in a chase, a wander and on the walk home, and at full speed again when the debuff ends or the player who put it on leaves or dies; a mob's own attack leap is not slowed. A slow ends at once (the game's half-second speed-up after a slow is not built yet), and no mob resists or is immune to a slow, a root or a stun yet (elites and bosses are held too). Roots, freezes, stuns, trips and sleeps work: a rooted mob takes no step (no chase, no wander, no walk home) but still attacks what is in its reach and turns to it, a frozen mob does the same without turning, and a stunned, tripped or sleeping mob also starts no attack, loses the attack it had begun (its hits that had not landed do not land) and does not turn; the leap of an attack that is under way is not cut by a root or a freeze, but a stun, a trip or a sleep stops it. The stun and trip animations are sent to the game (a trip over a stun plays the trip), and an effect ends with its time, the mob's death, the leaving or the death of the player who put it on, or the going of the pet whose hit put it on (what the player put on himself stays when a pet that refreshed it goes). A sleeping mob wakes at the first hit that takes Health from it, as the game's data says (a Lullaby's hit and a poison's tick do not wake it), and goes for its target in that same moment; a root, a freeze, a stun and a trip are not broken by damage. A hit's list of effects to remove is followed on a mob, by an ability's hit and by an effect's tick alike: the lockout of a slow takes the chill and the snare off once its build-up reaches 24, which a chill kept up with a Frostbolt every 3 seconds reaches in 11 seconds, and the lockouts of the other holds end a root or a freeze that was put on for long after 7.5 seconds, a stun or a trip after 4.5 seconds and a sleep after 14 seconds. An ability's hit works out the effects it puts on a mob before it takes its list off, so a Snipe on a bleeding mob turns the bleed into a Hemorrhage, and a Power Shot takes the bleed off with no Hemorrhage, as its data says. Knockbacks, pulls and pushes (the Cone of Cold's among them), silences and disarms are left off, so a mob does not take them. A root on a mob has not been seen in the game yet (it passed only its tests); a stunned mob's cooldown is not refunded, and the stun and trip animation's end time is modelled. | None. | Open: knockbacks and pulls (CC), the slow that Air Strike's root should take off, then the mob's attack speed. |

@known-issues set Abilities / Timing
| **Abilities / Timing** | Charged shots and spells take their mana and cooldown and land their damage at the moments the recorded game shows (Snipe, Power Shot, Thundering Shot, Headshot, Fireball and most abilities that need a target). The cast bars of 24 spells (Fireball, Frostbolt, Judgment, Mortal Blast, Smite and others) follow the end of your own bar: the mana, the cooldown and the damage come when your bar ends, whatever its length. A bar you cancel costs nothing and fires nothing when the game ends the cast, which it did for every cancel tried (modelled: no recording of the real game shows a cancelled bar); a Smite or an Arcane Circle, which pay at the start of their bar, give their mana and cooldown back. A Dodge that the server takes cancels an ability that has not gone out yet and gives back what it had paid (the mana, the charge or cooldown, the stacks of an effect it took). An ability has gone out once a hit of it was asked for or landed, an arrow or a volley left, or an effect of its own started. From then on the Dodge changes nothing: it does not cancel, refund or end the ability, and arrows already in the air still hit. A heal that has landed costs full. This holds for the cast bars (until the bar ends), the Salvo (until its first arrow leaves), the Barrage, the Air Strike and the Raining Death (until their first arrow or volley leaves), the Whirlwind (until it spins, about a third of a second after the press), Guard and Reflect (until their effects start, about a tenth of a second after the press, so by hand a Dodge never ends them), the Resplendent Beam (until you let go of the key), the Deliverance and the Combust (until the hit is asked for) and the weapon swings (until the first arrow leaves or the hit is asked for). A Dodge that the server refuses changes nothing. Bard songs and the Sprint go on through a Dodge. A bar that the game neither ends nor asks for goes on at a deadline two and a half times as long as the recorded bar, as if it had ended there. The Lightning Strike and the Resplendent Beam fire when you let go before the bar is full, at the charge reached (the Lightning Strike deals 100% to 300%; the charge is the time you held the key over your longest full bar, an estimate), and a tap fires at the least charge. The bars of Deliverance and Disengage, and the arrow storms, are not covered. The spells with a projectile land when it gets to the target, at any distance: the Arcane Volley, the Fireball, the Frostbolt, the Soul Flare, the Snipe, the Power Shot, the Headshot, the Thundering Shot, the Firebolt, the Dimensional Bolt, the Tomahawk, the Grapple, the Mock, the Quick Wit and the Jest, the Sleep Dart, the Throw, the Salvo's arrows, the Mend, the Discordance's three notes with their bounces and the ranged weapon swings of the Wand, the Shortbow, the Longbow and the Book (a volley of the Shortbow or the Book lands a number for each arrow; the Judgment's damage on an enemy comes with its cost step, as in the recordings). The Caltrops (thrown at the ground) does nothing yet, and the Bear Trap, the Saga and the pets' bolts still land at an average time; the Wand's beam steps and the melee swings keep their plain delay. The Discordance's notes, the Mend, the Salvo's arrows and the swings' arrows turn toward their target at a limited speed in the game, so a target beside or behind you is reached later than here, where they fly straight. The Salvo's shot rhythm and the end of its loop follow the character's animation speed by a model, not by a recording: the recorded Salvos ran at a speed of about 1. The Frostbolt's second bolt (the shard, which needs a talent and a Shocked target) does not fire yet. The launch uses the play rate the character's speed stat gives (checked against the game's own rate for two characters; the Bard's, the Rogue's and the Cleric's are by class; a weapon swing plays at the stat of the character's class, whatever the weapon), and the ability items of these abilities carry that rate. The mana and the cooldown still come at the recorded time, which for a slow character is before the missile leaves. | None. | Open. |

A pet's root, stun, trip, sleep and slow land on a mob (Stomp's talents, Incapacitate's hold) and end when the pet is dismissed or dies, but its knockbacks do not; the debuffs a pet puts on a mob (Demoralized, Volatile) change its numbers as they do for a player's hit, and a pet's hits do not shorten a Hex. @known-issues set Combat / Finishers
| **Combat / Finishers** | The game's reactions run for the events of your own hits and of paying an ability's cost, and only the Bard's have been played through. The other classes' reactions (Fighter Momentum, Mage essences and Tank Courage among them) run from the game's data where their shape is served and have not been checked against the game yet, so a number, a buff or a resource may differ from the game's. The weapon talents that react at a finisher (the wand's Burning, Chilled and Volatile, the axes' Bleed, Snare and Wound, and the like) fire at the deadly finishing hit of the weapon in hand; the debuffs they put on a mob (the wand's Burning and Volatile, the axes' Bleed and Wound, the scepter's Concussion and the like) are put on it, and the slows they put on a mob (Chilled, Snare) go through the same slow handling as the other slows (see Combat / Mob debuffs), which has not been tried with a finisher yet. Reactions that wait for you being hit (reflect, vengeance and the like) or for other events (an effect, a projectile, a lingering zone), that are cast by the hit's instigator (when it is not the holder of the effect that carries them), or that fire further hits at the allies around do nothing yet. The Mage's Shatter fires: lightning that takes Health from a Frozen mob lands the Shatter hit on it. | `--no-reactions` (or `AOC_NO_REACTIONS=1`) turns all reactions off. | Open: checking the other classes against the game, and the slows their finishers put on mobs. |
