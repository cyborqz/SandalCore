# SandalCore — Known Issues

This document lists the known issues of the current SandalCore main in one place: what happens, a workaround when there is one, and the status. What is still to be built is in [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md), under "Achieved and left".

**Last updated:** 2 November 2026

---

## Known Issues

| Area | What Happens | Workaround | Status |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quests** | The beginner quests can be finished up to the first one that needs something that is not built: Welcome to Verra (both ways) and Emberbound play to the end, the objects of the quests (bodies, crates, roots, building sites) stand in the world and can be used, and the items a quest asks for can be handed in to a person at the end of a conversation (Hungry For More: the Suspicious Bread to Cleric Beyam). Not built: a hand-in that asks for items by tag or rarity, and the pendant of The Great Mirage, which no conversation takes. The Harbinger quests need objects that stand in the map itself. Quest items arrive in the item bag, not the quest bag. The reward boxes some quests give arrive as items, but opening one needs the choose-one reward window, which is not built. | A game master can finish a quest with `questctl`. | Open: the objects in the map, the quest bag and the reward window. |
| **Quests / Givers** | Most quest givers are not in the world yet: of the 309 in the game's data only about 50 stand in the open world and the towns; the others are not placed there (quests and events spawn them, or they belong to other regions), so their quests are not offered. Quests that start from items are not offered either. | None. | Open: the givers that quests and events spawn; item quests later. |
| **Quests / Map markers** | Every active objective with a place gets a pin or an area when it starts: the people a talk objective names, the places of a "go to" objective, what a collect, use or hand-in objective tracks, the camps of mobs to kill. Not drawn yet: kill objectives named only by tags or whose mobs spawn at random in an area, the spawn-socket places of a "go to" objective, and the speech bubbles over NPCs. A pin is made once, when its objective starts, so it does not follow a walking NPC, and an NPC the world does not spawn has no pin. The quest-available mark shows on the NPCs within about 360 m that have a quest you can take; when an NPC offers several, the first one in the game's order decides which mark it shows. | The tracker's text says where to go. | Open: the speech bubbles, tag-named and random-area kills. |
| **Crates** | A construction crate is made at a Crate Packing station (13 of the 14 kinds: the Animal Husbandry crate needs a trained animal, which is not a material yet) and a trade crate at the commodity tables, and carried on the back in the cargo slot, which halves the carrier's speed. A trade crate's rarity follows the rarity of its materials (a construction crate is always Common), and a crate a quest gives is carried the same way. The Drop Crate ability is put on the action bar and takes the crate off the back, but into the item bag: the crate does not lie on the ground for others to pick up yet. A crate cannot be put into a building site or sold yet. The citizen table is paid in node currency, which a character cannot earn yet. | A game master gives a crate with `worldctl give-item` and money with `worldctl give-money`. | Next: the crate on the ground, then the building sites. |
| **Crafting / Artisan ratings** | A crafter's artisan ratings (quality, time, extra units, rarity and experience of the crafting and processing professions) come from the artisan gear worn in the shirt, pants and tool belt slots and from the scrolls and elixirs in effect, and from nothing else yet: food and drinks give none, and there are no guilds. The crafting cost and fuel cost ratings are left-overs in the game's data and do nothing. | Wear the artisan gear and use the scroll or elixir before you start a job: a job keeps the ratings it was started with, also after a login. | Planned: food and drinks, and guild perks. |
| **Consumables** | Scrolls and elixirs work from the item bag: one is taken from the stack, the 1 s cooldown runs, and the buff lasts for the item's own time (300 s to 10,800 s for scrolls, an hour for elixirs) and raises the stats and artisan ratings by the item's own numbers at its quality; a second scroll (or elixir) replaces the first. Food, drinks, potions and salves do nothing when used yet, and a buff ends at logout: it is not saved. | None. | Planned: food and drinks, potions and salves, and buffs that stay through a logout. |
| **Glint / Shops** | Shops and the trade crate tables take only the Glint kind a price names, so paying with a bigger kind (for example a Dim for a Dull price) is refused. | Keep some Dull Glint. | Planned: paying with change. |
| **Embersprings / Death window** | The death window does not list the first Emberspring a character discovered (for a new character, the Aelan Divine Gate). | Revive brings you back at the starting area. | Open. |
| **Weapons / Talents** | Weapon lines are paid by kills and by the game master level scrolls; quests and gathering give no weapon experience yet, and a line needs thousands of kills to reach level 25. The level-10 scroll leaves a line on level 9 and the level-20 scroll on level 19: the game's own numbers for the scrolls are a little under the lines' curve. | A game master sets a line with `worldctl weapon-xp <character> <line> <level>`. | Open: weapon experience from quests and gathering. |
| **Combat / Finishers** | The game's reactions run for the events of your own hits and of paying an ability's cost, and only the Bard's have been played through. The other classes' reactions (Fighter Momentum, Mage essences and Tank Courage among them) run from the game's data where their shape is served and have not been checked against the game yet, so a number, a buff or a resource may differ from the game's. The weapon talents that react at a finisher (the wand's Burning, Chilled and Volatile, the axes' Bleed, Snare and Wound, and the like) fire at the deadly finishing hit of the weapon in hand; the debuffs they put on a mob (the wand's Burning and Volatile, the axes' Bleed and Wound, the scepter's Concussion and the like) are put on it, and the slows they put on a mob (Chilled, Snare) are not yet (see Combat / Mob debuffs). Reactions that wait for you being hit (reflect, vengeance and the like) or for other events (an effect, a projectile, a lingering zone), that are cast by the hit's instigator, or that fire further hits at the allies around do nothing yet. | `--no-reactions` (or `AOC_NO_REACTIONS=1`) turns all reactions off. | Open: checking the other classes against the game, then the slows on mobs. |
| **Combat / Deadly finishers** | The deadly finisher of a weapon (the wand's beam among them) needs one of its Finisher talents and is then a chance the game rolls at each finishing swing: 25 % plus 10 % for each Proc Chance talent (35, 45 and 55 % with one, two and three), so with none one finishing swing in four ends in it. The Proc Chance talents' tooltips list 50, 75 and 100 %, which is more than the game uses. | None: it is the game's own roll. | As in the game. |
| **Abilities / Timing** | Charged shots and spells take their mana and cooldown and land their damage at the moments the recorded game shows (Snipe, Power Shot, Thundering Shot, Headshot, Fireball and most abilities that need a target). The cast bars of 24 spells (Fireball, Frostbolt, Judgment, Mortal Blast, Smite and others) follow the end of your own bar: the mana, the cooldown and the damage come when your bar ends, whatever its length. A bar you cancel costs nothing and fires nothing when the game ends the cast, which it did for every cancel tried (modelled: no recording of the real game shows a cancelled bar); a bar that the game neither ends nor asks for goes on at a deadline two and a half times as long as the recorded bar, as if it had ended there. The Lightning Strike and the Resplendent Beam fire when you let go before the bar is full, at the charge reached (the Lightning Strike deals 100% to 300%; the charge is the time you held the key over your longest full bar, an estimate), and a tap fires at the least charge. The bars of Deliverance and Disengage, and the arrow storms, are not covered. The spells with a projectile land when it gets to the target, at any distance: the Arcane Volley, the Fireball, the Frostbolt, the Soul Flare, the Snipe, the Power Shot, the Headshot, the Thundering Shot, the Firebolt, the Dimensional Bolt, the Tomahawk, the Grapple, the Mock, the Quick Wit and the Jest, the Sleep Dart, the Throw, the Salvo's arrows, the Mend and the Discordance's three notes with their bounces (the Judgment's damage on an enemy comes with its cost step, as in the recordings). The Caltrops (thrown at the ground) does nothing yet, and the Bear Trap, the Saga, the pets' bolts and the ranged weapon swings still land at an average time (Ball Lightning has its own row). The Discordance's notes, the Mend and the Salvo's arrows turn toward their target at a limited speed in the game, so a target beside or behind you is reached later than here, where they fly straight. The Salvo's shot rhythm and the end of its loop follow the character's animation speed by a model, not by a recording: the recorded Salvos ran at a speed of about 1. The Frostbolt's second bolt (the shard, which needs a talent and a Shocked target) does not fire yet. The launch uses the play rate the character's speed stat gives (checked against the game's own rate for two characters; the Bard's, the Rogue's and the Cleric's are by class), and the mana and the cooldown still come at the recorded time, which for a slow character is before the missile leaves. | None. | Open. |
| **Bard** | Resonance follows the game's rule: a Bard gains it from level 5, when the Resonant Weapon passive is granted, and none below. A finisher with the Menacing or Cathartic melody on adds its damage and spends up to 3 Resonance, and the Shocked and Wounded debuffs it puts on a mob are applied. The Cheerful, Pensive and Epic melodies do nothing at a finisher yet: no heal, no mana, no cleanse, and no Resonance spent. | None. | Open: the ally melodies. |
| **Rogue / Throw** | The Throw's shuriken flies and bounces to up to two other enemies, but it deals no damage and applies no Bleeding: its damage formula uses a form that the game-data compiler does not read yet. | None. | Open. |
| **Combat / Dice** | The hit, block and critical rolls repeat the same sequence after every server start. | None. | Open. |
| **Goblins / Flayers** | A Goblin Flayer's spinning attack plays where it stands: the Flayer does not follow its target while it spins, and does not sneer before it. | None. | Open. |
| **Goblins / Abilities** | Goblin Assassins do not rally, and Goblin Flame Shamans do not flee or put up their fire shield. | None. | Open. |
| **Bandits / Camps** | Only the bandit raiders, captains and arsonists are in the world. The archers and healers of their camps, and the cultists, are not, so the camps are thinner than in the game (the starting-area camp has 66 of its 95 bandits). | None. | Planned: their fights. |
| **Bandits / Arsonists** | An arsonist throws its fire bolt, and the few that have it its fire bolt combo, from up to 30 m and closes in to 25 m between casts, but it does not use its fire spray, its stream of fire or its ring of fire. A mob's projectile is not stopped by walls or other players on its way: it follows its target and lands when it arrives. | None. | Open: the arsonists' other fire attacks. |
| **Bandits / Fights** | A raider does not leap onto a player from a distance or walk while it whirls. A captain does not throw its tomahawk, boost its friends, taunt or raise its shield. The bandits of a camp do not join a fight together. | None. | Open. |
| **World / Day and night** | Day and night mobs follow the clock of the server's PC at start, not the sky. | None. | Open: one world clock. |
| **World / Walls and water** | Mobs can walk through fences and walls and along lake beds, and lava does no damage. | None. | Open. |
| **World / Biomes** | `GetNodeBiome()` returns undefined on certain boundary cells at the edge of the Riverlands, preventing regional reagent drops on edge spawns. | Stay within central Riverlands zone coordinates for node harvesting. | Adding boundary cell interpolation in region mapping. |
| **Inventory** | Dragging a stack onto another stack of the same item (to merge them) is refused, and so is splitting a stack. | Keep the stacks apart. | Planned: joining and splitting stacks. |
| **Towns** | The floor and the buildings of the five towns are in the walking map, but doors, stairs with high steps and thin awnings are not, a mount summoned indoors is not checked for room, and a town changed to another level keeps the buildings of the level it started at until the walking map is made again. Some guards and townspeople may still stand in the wrong place, and trees inside towns can't be cut. Nearby town NPCs of a kind with no attack animation yet can hit you with no visible attack (no animation or projectile); the town's wolves, Grems and ravens animate theirs. A long teleport into a town falls about 140 m before the safety net puts the player back, 4.5 to 6 seconds later. | Walk into a town from outside, or teleport to a point close to its streets. | Open: doors and stairs; swapping the walking map when a town changes level. |
| **Combat / Falling** | A fall does no damage, however far. | None. | Planned: fall damage. |
| **Abilities** | Chilling Laminate shows a placeholder blue circle on the ground below the player. | None. | Investigating which visual effect is sent for it. |
| **Ranger / Scatter Shot** | Scatter Shot can miss an enemy across a stream, a ledge or a bank: the server's line-of-sight check fails on such ground, so that enemy takes no damage. | Shoot from the same ground as the enemy. | Open. |
| **Ranger / Air Strike** | Air Strike hurts the enemies where its arrows land, but does not root (CC) one that a slow debuff is on (mobs do not slow down or stop for a slow, snare or CC yet). | None. | Open. |
| **Mage / Ball Lightning** | Ball Lightning plays and takes its cost, but does no damage: its slow ball, which hurts what it passes over, is not moved. | None. | Planned: the ball's path. |
| **Bard / Saga** | The Saga's projectiles (the heals and buffs of its stories that fly to allies) are not sent. | None. | Open. |
| **Summoner / Pets** | Summoner pets are not implemented yet: summoning does not bring out a pet. | None. | Planned: Summoner pets. |
| **Gathering / Speed** | Gathering takes longer than on retail. | None. | Investigating how the gathering speed ratings (skills, talents, tools and gear) apply. |
| **Gathering / Full bag** | When the resource bag is full, the extra items drop in an ash pile, the look used for death piles. | Make room in the resource bag before gathering. | Open: dropped items need a look of their own. |
| **Gathering / Restart** | Cut trees and hunted animals reset when the server restarts. | None. | Open: their state is not saved between runs. |
| **Mounts / Aquatic** | Attempting to mount or remount an aquatic mount while swimming in water fails to complete possession. | Mount on land or shallow shoreline before entering deep water. | Investigating water volume entry state during mount possession. |
| **Mounts / Flight** | 44 mayor-specific flying mounts fail client-side item validation (`ValidateItemUse`) unless node citizenship and mayorship criteria are satisfied. | Use the PTR Eagle Gryphon mounts, which bypass node tenure validation. | Tied to City Node election milestone completion. |
| **Combat / No navmesh** | No navmesh = no combat: every mob has 0 HP and damage does nothing. Cause: `worldd` starts its combat system only after `dist\content\area_nav.bin` (with `area_geom.bin`) has loaded, and these 1.2 GB files are built on your PC, not shipped. | Run `install.bat` (the launchers do the game data step too): it builds them from your Ashes of Creation install. It needs the game found (`GAME_ROOT` or `-GameRoot`), the .NET 8 SDK and a built `aoc_navbuild`. | Automatic: the `navmesh` step of `scripts\extract_game_data.ps1` builds it. |
| **Combat / Mob debuffs** | A mob takes the debuffs of a hit, the Burning debuff of a hit that sets it on fire (the Mage's Fireball with the Fire Empowerment, the wand's Burning finisher): a fire number over it every second for 8 seconds (up to 24 with more burning hits), with the fire and the Burning icon on it. The Burning debuff goes on through a reset, ends with the mob's death, and ends when the player who lit it leaves or dies. While a mob walks home after a reset its damage-over-time debuffs still hurt it but start no fight; once it is home every damage-over-time debuff that is still on it starts the fight again, so an attacker it cannot see makes it leash and heal again at each tick until the debuff ends. The bleeds and poisons of a hit take Health from a mob at their ticks: a Bleeding debuff every second for 6 seconds, a Hemorrhage every half second for 5 seconds, a Poison every 2 seconds for 20 seconds with its stacks (up to ten) making each tick bigger (modelled, not confirmed: each tick counts every stack); a mob has one Bleeding debuff for each attacker, and a Hemorrhage only lands on a bleeding mob. The debuffs that change the mob's own numbers work: Shaken makes it take more physical damage and Volatile more magical damage, Demoralized makes it hit softer, Blinded makes it miss more, and a new hit adds time to them up to 15 or 20 seconds. Weakened and Dazed lower the mob's physical and magical speed numbers, but a mob does not attack slower for them yet. Slows, snares and CC (roots, stuns) are left off, so a mob does not slow down or stop for one. | None. | Open: slows, snares and CC (roots, stuns), then the mob's attack speed. |
| **Mobs / Packs** | Mobs next to one that noticed you do not join the fight until they notice you themselves. | None. | Open: pack aggro. |
| **Mobs / Damage** | Some mobs hit harder or softer than in retail (deer and rams about three times as hard, bears almost twice). | None. | Open: their stats are compared with retail first. |
| **Mobs / Fights** | About half of the hostile mobs (undead, Minotaurs, constructs, spiders and others) use a generic attack list instead of their own, and the goblin shamans' bomb flies but leaves no ooze on the ground (the flame shamans' fire bombs are not thrown) and the goblins do not rally yet. | None. | Open. |
| **Server / Many players** | In a simulated load test with players that only walk in one place, one world server kept its 30 updates a second, each within 50 ms of its time, with a few dozen simulated players, and began to fall behind at about that size (updates then arrived up to 0.1 s late). Players who fight and ride cost more, and the server has not been measured with a real crowd. | Fewer players in one place. | Open: faster updates are planned. |
